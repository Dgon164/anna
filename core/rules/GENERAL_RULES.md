# The general rules — knowledge earned on past jobs, valid for all future ones
*Each rule was bought with a demonstration. Date and origin noted. These are
the "generalized discoverable rules" that live in her core for future
assessment and use (owner spec, 2026-08-12). New rules are promoted here only
when they are general — true of tables, never of one client's numbers.*

1. **Orders, not rows** (2026-07-29, the blind test on real retail data).
   A 20-line invoice is ONE visit. Counting rows as visits produced a false
   "93% repeat customers"; the true rate was ~19-25%.
2. **Subtract the base rate before naming a source** (2026-07-29). "The UK
   leads returns" is a non-answer when the UK is 95% of everything. A source
   is only news if its share of the problem beats its share of the whole.
3. **Caps degrade to top-N, never to silence** (2026-07-30). When a list is
   too long to show, show the strongest few and say what was cut — never
   show nothing.
4. **Two kinds of claims, never blended** (2026-07-28, owner ruling). A
   certified rule passed the engine's strictest checks; a what's-there
   description is graded by whether it survives 200 random half-draws. The
   report always says which is which.
5. **A claim about the reality must survive a fresh draw** (2026-08-12,
   owner ruling). True-of-this-sample and true-of-the-process are different
   statements. Weak coincidences may be reported as weak; stamping them
   durable is the failure.
6. **Score with the middle value, not the average** (2026-08-10, the star
   exam). Averages punish the strangers a true discovery creates and prefer
   the muddle; measured on the star file, the true correction looked WORSE
   under an average ruler and clearly better under the middle-value ruler.
7. **Trust is a ratio** (2026-08-10). A measurement's absolute error is not
   its trustworthiness; value-to-error is. Cutting on absolute error alone
   selected the wrong region.
8. **The leftover is never waste** (2026-08-12, charter). What remains after
   every accepted account is either structure not yet earned or the
   fingerprint of how the thing was made. Nothing is dropped; the leftover
   keeps the books balanced.
9. **Impossible values are a message from the instrument** (2026-08-12, the
   negatives check). Values that cannot physically exist (negative
   distances) are a free, labeled sample of pure measurement noise — they
   can certify or convict the stated error column from inside the table.
10. **The content is the rows, not their order** (2026-08-12, owner ruling).
    The same table fed in any sequence must produce the same readout — and
    the feed order itself is kept as a recoverable timeline column, because
    sometimes the order carries its own signal.
11. **A law's validity boundary is a finding** (2026-08-10). If a rule only
    holds where some third quantity is in range, the range is itself a
    discovery — usually the shadow of who was kept out of the data.
12. **A changing slope means missing rows, not a changing law** (2026-08-10).
    When tightening the cut moves the answer, the data is incomplete; track
    any drift within one fixed family only, never across whoever happens to
    be present.
